# Project Description

## Project name

Working name: **ClientFlow AI**

The name can change later. The important thing is the system structure.

## One-sentence description

ClientFlow AI is a multi-business automation platform that helps small businesses capture customer requests, respond instantly, follow up automatically, and manage leads from one dashboard.

## Problem

Small businesses lose customers because they reply late, forget follow-ups, miss messages, or do not have a simple system for tracking leads.

Many local businesses depend on Instagram DMs, WhatsApp messages, phone calls, Google Business Profile messages, or basic contact forms. These channels are useful, but they are not always organized.

Common problems:

- Customers ask questions, but the owner replies too late.
- Leads are scattered across Instagram, WhatsApp, email, and phone.
- Owners forget who they have already contacted.
- Customers do not receive immediate confirmation.
- Follow-ups are manual and inconsistent.
- Small businesses cannot afford complex CRM systems.

## Solution

ClientFlow AI gives each business a simple branded customer form, an owner dashboard, and automation tools.

When a customer submits a request:

1. The lead is saved.
2. AI summarizes the request.
3. The customer receives an instant confirmation email.
4. The business owner receives a notification.
5. The lead appears in the owner dashboard.
6. The system can schedule follow-ups.
7. The owner can mark the lead as new, contacted, booked, lost, or completed.

## Target customers

Start with simple service businesses.

Good first customers:

- Cleaning businesses
- Barbers and salons
- Caterers
- Photographers
- Tutors
- Repair services
- Event planners
- Small agencies
- Local consultants
- Campus organizations
- Churches and nonprofits

Avoid sensitive industries at first, such as medical, legal, financial advice, or anything that needs heavy compliance.

## Target user roles

### Super Admin

This is the platform owner. In the MVP, this is Cephas.

Responsibilities:

- Create businesses
- Invite owners
- Generate form links
- Manage business status
- View platform-wide activity
- Monitor emails and AI usage
- See audit logs

### Business Owner

This is the client using the platform.

Responsibilities:

- Log into their dashboard
- View leads
- Read AI summaries
- Update lead statuses
- Copy/share their public form link
- Manage business settings

### Customer

This is the person filling out the business form.

Responsibilities:

- Open the public business form link
- Submit request information
- Receive automatic confirmation

Customers do not need accounts.

## MVP features

### Admin portal

- Super admin login
- Create business
- Generate business slug
- Generate public form link
- Invite business owner
- View businesses
- Edit business details
- Activate/deactivate business
- View audit logs

### Owner dashboard

- Owner login
- View leads
- View lead details
- Read AI summaries
- Update lead status
- Copy public form link
- View basic business settings

### Public customer form

- Dynamic form based on business slug
- Branded with business name, logo, and color
- Collect name, email, phone, service needed, preferred time, and message
- Submit lead without customer login

### Automation

- AI lead summary using DeepSeek
- Customer auto-reply using Resend
- Owner new-lead notification using Resend
- Basic follow-up scheduling foundation

## Business model

Start as a paid setup service plus monthly subscription.

Possible pricing:

- Beta: $149–$299 setup + $49–$99/month
- After validation: $499 setup + $149–$299/month

The first goal is not to build a huge SaaS immediately. The first goal is to get businesses paying for a useful automation.
